About

Zichun Zhu is an AI engineer and a Computer Science PhD graduate from the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS) at The University of Queensland (UQ), with expertise in machine learning, spatiotemporal data, sequence modelling, and intelligent transportation systems.

Most recently, he led the AI transformation of an ERP software company. He redesigned its platform to be AI-ready, built an integrated AI agent and custom models, set up AI governance, and trained teams to work with AI coding tools.

His PhD research, part of an ARC Linkage Project with Transport and Main Roads and Brisbane City Council, developed deep learning, contrastive and semi-supervised methods for reconstructing vehicle trajectories from Bluetooth sensor data. He combines research-level machine learning with hands-on experience turning AI into real products.
